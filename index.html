31mm Excitement

A community hype meter for upcoming films: how excited people are to see them, not a review of quality. Open any film, set how excited you are on the slider (or tap on mobile), and every visitor's vote averages into the film's score, from 0 to 31mm.

  • 26–31Must-see hype
  • 22–25Highly anticipated
  • 16–21Keen to watch
  • 6–15Mild interest
