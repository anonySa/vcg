VCG Excitement

A community hype meter for upcoming films: how excited people are to see them, not a review of quality. Open any film, set how excited you are on the slider (or tap on mobile), and every visitor's vote averages into the film's tier.

  • S · 85-100% · Must-see hype
  • A · 70-84% · Highly anticipated
  • B · 50-69% · Keen to watch
  • C · 20-49% · Mild interest
  • F · under 20% · Not fussed
